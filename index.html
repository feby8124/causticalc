10 — HOW TO USE
🧭 GETTING STARTED

Process Lab is a set of six live calculators for chlor-alkali shift operations. Every tab works the same way: type a value into any input, and every result on that tab recalculates instantly — there's no "Calculate" button to press.

Fields with a light-green readonly background are computed automatically from the fields above them; you don't type into those directly.

⚙ NAOH DENSITY
  1. Enter the NaOH concentration (Wt%) and temperature.
  2. Read off density, specific gravity, and molarity instantly.
  3. The SVG chart plots density curves for 10–50 Wt% so you can see where your reading sits, with a marker at your current input.
⚙ NAOH MIXING
  1. Enter your source and target concentrations, plus the batch quantity.
  2. The mixing ratio bar shows the proportion of concentrated feed to dilution water at a glance.
  3. Use the water and feed quantities shown to dose the batch tank.
⚙ NAOCl (HYPO)
  1. Enter Cl₂ feed rate, NaOH strength, and target hypo strength.
  2. Stoichiometric NaOH and water demand are calculated automatically.
  3. Check the final g/L available chlorine before releasing the batch.
⚙ HCl 32%
  1. Enter Cl₂ input, purity, and target HCl Wt%.
  2. Pure and diluted HCl output, plus dilution water volume, update together.
  3. Use "Required HCl Output" to back-calculate the Cl₂ feed needed for a target batch size.
⚙ HYPO TANK BLENDING
  1. Enter the volume and available-chlorine concentration for Tank 1 and Tank 2.
  2. The tool runs a mass balance to give you the blended tank's final volume and g/L Cl₂.
  3. Cross-check the result against your logbook reading before dispatch.
🧪 LIQUID CHLORINE STORAGE
  1. Click any tank card (D-352A–D) to select it — the controls update to show that tank's level.
  2. Drag the slider or type a percentage to set the current level; the MT value updates instantly.
  3. Enter a previous level and hours elapsed to calculate the rate of change (filling / discharging).
  4. Use the 24hr and shift projections to plan production or receiving operations.
📈 TRENDS
  1. On any calculator tab, click 💾 Save Point to store the current inputs and results with a timestamp.
  2. Switch to the Trends tab, pick the calculator and metric you want to review.
  3. The chart and table below update to show that metric's history — hover a point for the exact value and time.
  4. Delete individual entries with the ✕ button, or clear all history for a calculator with the button below the table.
💡

All saved points live in this browser's local storage only. They stay on this device and aren't synced to any server — clearing your browser data will erase them.
